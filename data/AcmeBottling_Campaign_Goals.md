# Acme Bottling - Campaign Input Brief (Sample)

**Product to promote:** SKU-PET500 - 500 mL PET Bottle (new lightweight, 100% recyclable line)
**Launch window:** Q4 2026
**Primary audience:** Mid-size beverage brands (juice, water, kombucha) evaluating sustainable packaging.
**Key customers to feature:** Sunrise Juice Co. (CUST-1002), Blue Ridge Springs (CUST-1004), Prairie Kombucha (CUST-1005).

## Goals
- Position the new PET line as lighter, lower-cost, and fully recyclable.
- Drive 40 qualified sales conversations in Q4.
- Support the sales team with a one-pager and an executive pitch deck.

## Proof points (approved)
- 18% lighter than the previous PET bottle.
- 100% recyclable resin from a certified supplier.
- Unit cost 30% below comparable glass (SKU-GB16).

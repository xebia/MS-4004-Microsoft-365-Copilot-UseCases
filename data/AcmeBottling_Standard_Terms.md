# Acme Bottling — Standard Contract Language (Reference Playbook)

Use this as the approved baseline. When reviewing a customer contract, compare against
these clauses and flag anything missing, weaker, or non-standard.

## 1. Payment Terms
Standard: Net 30 from invoice date. Late payments accrue 1.5% monthly interest.

## 2. Delivery & Risk of Loss
Standard: FOB Acme facility. Risk of loss passes to customer at carrier pickup.

## 3. Limitation of Liability
Standard: Acme's total liability capped at the value of the affected order. No liability
for indirect or consequential damages.

## 4. Warranty
Standard: Products warranted free of manufacturing defects for 90 days from delivery.
Sole remedy is replacement or credit.

## 5. Force Majeure
Standard: Neither party liable for delays due to events beyond reasonable control
(supply shortage, natural disaster, labor action).

## 6. Termination
Standard: Either party may terminate with 60 days written notice. Outstanding orders
honored through termination date.

## 7. Confidentiality
Standard: Mutual confidentiality for 3 years covering pricing, specs, and volumes.

## 8. Governing Law
Standard: Governed by the laws of the State of Ohio.

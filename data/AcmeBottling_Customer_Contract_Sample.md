# Sample Customer Contract — Northwind Distilling (CUST-1003)
### (Sanitized for training — fictional)

**Supply Agreement between Acme Bottling Co. and Northwind Distilling**

**1. Payment Terms.** Customer shall pay all invoices Net 45 from invoice date.
(No late-interest provision included.)

**2. Delivery.** Delivery FOB customer receiving dock. Risk of loss passes on delivery
to customer dock.

**3. Limitation of Liability.** (Section left blank in this draft.)

**4. Warranty.** Products warranted free of manufacturing defects for 30 days from
delivery.

**5. Force Majeure.** Neither party liable for delays due to events beyond reasonable
control.

**6. Termination.** Either party may terminate with 30 days written notice.

**7. Confidentiality.** Mutual confidentiality for 1 year.

**8. Governing Law.** Governed by the laws of the State of Wisconsin.

**9. Exclusivity.** Customer agrees to purchase 100% of glass bottle requirements from
Acme for the contract term. (Non-standard clause — not in Acme's baseline.)
